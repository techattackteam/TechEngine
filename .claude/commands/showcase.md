Act as my product owner's analyst, planning the showcase video for: $ARGUMENTS

A showcase is part of its goal's Definition of Done ([[Channel]]). Its viewers are developers who
might build a game with TechEngine one day, so it shows what works and why it matters to them.

**When this runs:** at the `/sprint-prep` for the sprint that ends the goal, so the video card has
an outline, or when I am about to record.

## Gather

- The goal's row on `docs/02 Roadmap/Roadmap.md`, and the system's brief and Capability Map
  section in `docs/01 Product/`.
- The goal's cards and their Done entries in the sprint notes, for what actually shipped.
- `docs/01 Product/Channel.md`, and the earlier outlines in `docs/01 Product/Showcases/`, so
  videos stay consistent and none repeats another.

## Steps

1. **The takeaway**: one sentence a developer should remember after watching.
2. **The beats**: four to eight moments, in order, each something **visible on screen**, each
   tied to a *Must* from the brief.
3. **What to say**: two or three plain points per beat. No hype, no promises.
4. **What comes next**: one line that points at the next goal.
5. **A title and a thumbnail idea**: two options each.
6. **Shorts**: moments from the beats that would work on their own.
7. **Check every beat against what merged.** A beat that needs unmerged or unverified work is
   marked, never written as if it works.

## Write

- The outline to `docs/01 Product/Showcases/<goal> - <title>.md`.
- A row in [[Channel]] § *Videos*: goal, working title, status *planned*. When I publish, I give
  you the link and the row becomes *published*.

## Guardrails

- **Never show or claim a feature that has not merged.** The video is evidence; a false claim is
  worse than a shorter video.
- Recording, editing and publishing are mine. Attended only. Vault only. Do not commit unless I
  ask.
